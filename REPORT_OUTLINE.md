# BIT Mesra Project Report Outline

This outline is adapted from the uploaded **Project Report Template.pdf**. The template lists a maximum of five chapters:

1. Introduction — project overview.
2. Literature Review — project background and related work.
3. Methodology — methods and algorithms applied.
4. Experimental Results and Discussion — results and analysis.
5. Conclusion — accomplished work and future scope.

## Front matter from the template

- Cover/title page: project title, B.Tech., branch, student name(s), roll number(s), institute.
- Approval of guide.
- Declaration certificate.
- Certificate of approval.
- Abstract (maximum 250 words; no citations in abstract).
- Acknowledgement (maximum 300 words).
- Contents.
- List of figures and list of tables.

## Formatting checklist from the uploaded template

- A4 page size; normal margins of 2.54 cm on all sides.
- Times New Roman throughout.
- Body text 12 pt, justified, 1.5 line spacing; no paragraph indent required.
- Report title: 18 pt bold, title case.
- Chapter title: 18 pt bold italic, title case.
- Section heading: 16 pt bold; subsection heading: 14 pt bold.
- Figures/tables captions: 12 pt; footnotes: 9 pt.
- Each chapter starts on a new page.
- Maximum 30 pages for the report body, excluding cover/front matter, lists, acknowledgement and annexures.
- Plagiarism checking is mandatory; follow the guide's current instructions and attach the required report.

Confirm the department's current requirements with the project guide before final formatting. The supplied template includes some internship-report wording; update only with guide approval.
