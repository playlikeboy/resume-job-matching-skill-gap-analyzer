# BIT Mesra Project Presentation Outline

The uploaded **Guidelines for Project Presentation.pdf** requires 12–15 slides including the title slide. The title slide must include project title, student name and roll number, and guide name and affiliation. Text should be in bullet form, and images from external resources must be cited.

## Suggested 14-slide deck

1. **Title** — project title, team names/roll numbers, guide and affiliation.
2. **Motivation** — why manual resume-to-job comparison is difficult.
3. **Problem statement** — keyword mismatch, missing evidence and lack of actionable feedback.
4. **Objectives and scope** — target role families and what is deliberately deferred.
5. **Related work** — resume parsing, skill extraction, embeddings and explainability.
6. **Research gap and contribution** — what will be tested, not presumed.
7. **Dataset and annotation** — sources, privacy, labels and split design.
8. **System architecture** — resume/JD input through parsing, matching and dashboard.
9. **Methodology** — taxonomy, required/preferred weights, match labels and formulas.
10. **Tools and techniques** — React, FastAPI, PyMuPDF, spaCy/taxonomy, Sentence Transformers.
11. **Application demo** — sample input and evidence-based result.
12. **Results and discussion** — baseline vs hybrid metrics and error analysis (real results only).
13. **Conclusion and future work** — findings, limitations, OCR, broader roles and consented portfolio evidence.
14. **References / Q&A** — topic-specific references and acknowledgements.

## Presentation checklist

- Keep the deck within 12–15 slides including title.
- Include separate slides for objectives, motivation/background, methodology (preferably graphical), tools/techniques, results/discussion, conclusion/future work and references.
- Use bullets rather than paragraph blocks.
- Cite all e-resource images and external figures on the references slide.
- Use readable fonts, clear charts and precisely labelled diagrams.
